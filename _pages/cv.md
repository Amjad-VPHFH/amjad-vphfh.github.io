**Research Project (June 2025–July 2026):** Co-Contamination of Fusarium Mycotoxins and Probiotic *Bacillus cereus* in Animal Feed and Feed Ingredients

* Investigated the biodegradation and biosorption potential of probiotic *B. cereus* against selected mycotoxins.
