**Status:** MS thesis, completed 2026 (presented at BSVER ASCon XXXII)

**Supervisor:** Dr. Md. Atiqul Haque, HSTU

**Question:** How common is DON contamination in commercial feeds and feed ingredients, and which factors are associated with it?

**Methods:** Field sampling, ELISA-based DON quantification, fungal culture, DNA extraction, PCR, and risk assessment.

**Key findings:**

- Of 84 samples analyzed, 24 (28.57%) exceeded the permissible DON limit and 60 were within acceptable levels.
- Prevalence was higher in summer (42.86%) than in winter (14.29%).
- Across the four feed types (21 samples each), broiler and fish feeds had the highest prevalence (42.86%, 9/21 each), while cattle and layer feeds had the lowest (14.29%, 3/21 each).
