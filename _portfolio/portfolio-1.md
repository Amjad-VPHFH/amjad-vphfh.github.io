**Status:** MS thesis, completed 2026 (presented at BSVER ASCon XXXII)
**Supervisor:** Dr. Md. Atiqul Haque, HSTU

**Question:** How common is DON contamination in commercial feeds and feed ingredients, and which factors are associated with it?

**Methods:** Field sampling, ELISA-based DON quantification, fungal culture, DNA extraction, PCR, and risk assessment.

**Key findings:** The analysis of 84 samples revealed an overall Deoxynivalenol (DON) prevalence of 28.57%, with 24 samples exceeding the permissible limit and 60 falling within acceptable levels. while summer encountered 42.86% and winter 14.29% .• Among the four feed types tested (21 samples each), broiler and fish feeds showed the highest prevalence rates at 42.86% (9/21 samples each). In contrast, cattle and layer feeds had significantly lower prevalence rates at 14.29% (3/21 samples each).
